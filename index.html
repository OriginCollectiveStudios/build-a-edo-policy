Terms of Service

Effective 6 November 2026

What the app does

Build A Edo Helper is a private tool used by the creator of Build A Edo, an animated series about building old Tokyo (Edo) together with its followers. It sends the creator’s own finished videos to the drafts of the Build A Edo TikTok account. The creator then adds a sound and publishes each video by hand in the TikTok app.

Who can use it

The app is used only by the owner of the Build A Edo TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not post on anyone else’s account.

Content

  • Only original Build A Edo videos made by the creator are uploaded.
  • Videos go to drafts only. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Every upload follows TikTok’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Changes and contact

These terms may be updated when the tool changes; the date above shows the latest version. Questions can be sent as a message to @build.a.edo on Instagram or @buildaedo on TikTok.

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

What we collect

  • The TikTok account ID and display name of the Build A Edo account, to make sure uploads only go to that account.
  • The access token TikTok gives after the owner approves the app, used only to send videos to drafts.

What we do not collect

The app does not read, store or share data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user. It does not collect messages, contacts or location.

How it is stored

The access token is kept in a private file on the creator’s own working machine, never in public code and never shared. The owner can remove access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Contact

Message @build.a.edo on Instagram or @buildaedo on TikTok for any privacy question.
